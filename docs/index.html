Privacy Policy — Timely Quote

Last updated: 4 September 2026

The short version: Timely Quote does not collect or process personal information. There is no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information Timely Quote collects

None. Timely Quote does not ask for personal information, create a user profile, or send usage information to the developer or an analytics provider.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers the passage last shown, your appearance preference, and whether automatic refresh is enabled. These preferences stay on your device. The literary passages are supplied as a read-only library within the app.

Sharing and external links

Timely Quote can open a book on Project Gutenberg and can pass a quote to the system share sheet. These actions happen only when you choose them. Once you leave Timely Quote or select a sharing destination, the privacy policy of that website or service applies.

Your control

Deleting Timely Quote removes its locally stored app data from your device, subject to the normal behavior of any device backups you control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the current version will remain available on this page.

Timely Quote includes passages from public-domain books made available by Project Gutenberg. Project Gutenberg is not affiliated with Timely Quote.
